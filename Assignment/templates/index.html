PMRL Grammar

Context-free grammar used by the recursive descent parser. 

PROGRAM       → STATEMENT*

STATEMENT     → ASSIGNMENT
              | IF_STATEMENT

ASSIGNMENT    → IDENTIFIER "=" EXPRESSION ";"

IF_STATEMENT  → "if" CONDITION "then"
                STATEMENT
                "else"
                STATEMENT

CONDITION     → EXPRESSION RELATIONAL_OP EXPRESSION

EXPRESSION    → TERM
              | EXPRESSION "+" TERM
              | EXPRESSION "-" TERM

TERM          → FACTOR
              | TERM "*" FACTOR
              | TERM "/" FACTOR

FACTOR        → IDENTIFIER
              | INTEGER
              | "(" EXPRESSION ")"

RELATIONAL_OP → "<" | ">" | "<=" | ">="
              | "==" | "!="
